The resort

Welcome to the canyon

East Canyon Resort is a member-owned mountain resort near Morgan, Utah. Members and guests enjoy RV camping, furnished condos, and wilderness camping, plus swimming pools and hot tubs, sports courts, mini-golf, fishing, hiking, and many miles of ATV trails.

The resort sits near East Canyon Reservoir for boating, swimming, and fishing, and is within driving distance of the Weber River and several Utah ski areas. Condos may be rented by non-members, and the resort hosts weddings and special events.

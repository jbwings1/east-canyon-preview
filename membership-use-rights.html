Membership Use Rights

Summary of Regular Time, Bonus Time, RV Use, and Relative Rights by membership class. Source: Policies §3.2 (Member Classes) and §2.10–2.11 (Summer / Winter seasons).

Seasons

  • Summer: May 15 – September 15
  • Winter: September 16 – May 14

Use rights by membership type

Numbers are Regular Time days per season unless noted. A dash (—) means none for that season or right.

Notes

  • H / I Corporate: Each year the corporation may designate up to four officers, partners, directors, trustees, or employees as Designated Members. Designated Members have Bonus Time and RV Use with occupancy rules in §3.2 (only one may occupy as a Member in wilderness campsites, RV Park, or condos at a time; all four may be on site for day use).
  • Relative Rights Member: Same Use Rights and Privileges as the associated Full Member under §3, except no Regular Time, hunting rights, or guest reservations / guests. Bonus Time may be scheduled up to 21 days in advance. Term runs April 1 – March 31 with an annual fee (see Appendix B).
  • Temporary Member Condo / FRA: Temporary use rights for condo or FRA stays as defined in §3.2.15; not a Full Member class in the table above.
  • * Bonus Time is subject to space available and other reservation rules in the Policies and Rules.

This page is a quick reference only. The official text controls: §3.2 Member Classes and §2.10–2.11. See also Policies and Rules.

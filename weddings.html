Weddings

With over 9,000 acres of wilderness about 16 miles from Park City, East Canyon Resort offers destination-wedding settings.

Weddings at the Lodge

For a rustic experience with a little less adventure, there is a ceremony site along the creek behind the lodge.

Mountain-top Ceremonies

Several ceremony sites with mountain-top views are offered. Availability of packages can change – ask Special Events for current details.

Indoor Weddings

The lodge can serve as a backdrop for an indoor wedding, with versatile space for different ceremony styles.

Bridal Suite

The Bridal Suite includes a full kitchen, two private restrooms, a sitting area next to the fireplace, 10 lighted vanity mirrors, two full-length mirrors, a clothing rack, and other details for wedding-day preparation.

For wedding planning, contact Special Events at 801-359-9030 option 2, specialevents@eastcanyon.com, or Contact Information.

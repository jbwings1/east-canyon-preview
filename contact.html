Directions from Salt Lake International Airport

Summer

Take I-80 East. After a brief crossover on I-15 South, continue east on I-80. Travel up Parley’s Canyon on I-80 East to the East Canyon Exit 134, Highway 65. Turn left at the end of the exit and stay on Highway 65 for 14 miles. Highway 65 goes to the top of Big Mountain and then drops down to East Canyon. The resort is on your left.

Winter

Take I-80 East. After a brief crossover on I-15 South, continue east on I-80. Travel past Park City and Echo Reservoir and take I-84 West, exit 168. Take exit 115 at Henefer and turn left. In Henefer, follow the signs and turn left onto Highway 65 South. After about 8 miles, turn left at the fork in the road. Continue on Highway 65 for 4.5 miles to East Canyon Resort; the resort is on the right. Highway 65 North over Big Mountain is closed from about November 15 through May 15. The resort remains accessible through Henefer or Morgan during the closure.

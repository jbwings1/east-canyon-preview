Visitor information

East Canyon Resort is a member-owned mountain resort on about 9,600 acres near Morgan, Utah. It is around 15 miles from Henefer or Morgan, Utah to the resort. It offers RV camping, condominium stays, and wilderness camping on private land.

East Canyon Resort includes 81 RV spots with full hookups, 32 condominiums, and 3 family reunion sites. Members and guests can also use recreation amenities such as swimming pools and hot tubs, sports courts (including tennis, pickleball, basketball, and volleyball), mini-golf, a game room, playgrounds, fishing, hiking, and many miles of ATV trails. Condos may also be rented by non-members. Opportunities are available for weddings and special events.

The resort is near East Canyon Reservoir for boating, swimming, and fishing, and is within driving distance of the Weber River and several Utah ski areas.

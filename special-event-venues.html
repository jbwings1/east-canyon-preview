Special Event Venues

East Canyon Resort offers indoor and outdoor venues for birthday parties, company retreats, and other events. Guests may also use Resort amenities while attending an event. The Special Events Coordinator can help plan your event at 801-359-9030, option 2, specialevents@eastcanyon.com, or Contact Information. Weddings and wedding receptions are priced differently — see the Weddings page.

The Loft

An open, spacious room with rustic décor, warm lighting, a fireplace, and beautiful flooring.

Capacity: 150 people seated

The Deck

A large deck overlooking a scenic lawn, with a river and lush green wilderness.

Capacity: 150 people seated

Pavilion

The Party Pavilion is suited for a birthday party or barbecue. It is located near the mini-golf and tennis court.

Capacity: around 70 people seated

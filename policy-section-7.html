7. RV STORAGE AREAS

Previous | Next

7.1. Long Term

Based on space availability and first come-first served, Members may park one (1) RV and one (1) utility trailer or boat with trailer per Membership Unit in the Long Term Storage Area as long as the vehicle(s) have a current state license, are in working condition, have an ECR sticker and an ECR Long Term Parking Permit. Ownership papers will be required. If the Unit has not been utilized and/or moved during the period of March 31st to April 1st of the following year, the Member may be subject to a fine and notice requiring removal of the Unit. Any noncomplying equipment or vehicle may be removed for salvage or auction. See Appendix A for fee. Amended November 14, 2024.

7.2. Winter Storage

It is recommended that all boats and RVs be removed from the Long Term Storage Area before Winter because of the harsh winter conditions at the Resort. The Resort is not obligated to remove snow from

the RV Storage Area access road to permit owner's access to their RV, boat, or other property stored in the area.

7.3. Liability

ECR is not liable for any damage, loss or theft which may occur to property stored in any storage area or parked anywhere on ECR property.

7.4. Short Term

Members may use Short Term Storage for up to seven (7) consecutive nights with no charge. A Daily Short Term Storage Fee will be charged for every additional consecutive night after the first seven (7) days. After fourteen (14) consecutive days, all non-complying vehicles/equipment may be towed at owner's expense. See Appendix A for penalty. See Appendix B for fee.

7.5. Parking Pass

All vehicles parked in Short Term Storage or Long Term Storage must display a valid Parking Pass for that date and location. Non-complying vehicles/equipment may be towed at owner's expense, or impounded with a tire locking device. See Appendix A for penalty.

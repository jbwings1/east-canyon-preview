Make a reservation

This area is for resort administrators. Admin sign in

On the phone with a member? Use Sites taken first to see what is open, then make the reservation below. Manage reservations is the list of existing bookings.

Sites taken

Lookup only — this does not make a reservation. Pick any from and to dates to see everything taken in that range. Green is open, red is booked, and blue is a stay on the signed-in admin account when it appears. Click a colored site for booked dates in your range — details appear beside the map. You can also see member names and booking details on each site.

Reservation for a member

You cannot book for yourself while signed in as admin. Type a name or member number and choose the member from the list that opens under the field. Pick the type and stay dates — the booking map shows which sites are open for those dates. Click a green site to select it. For an RV stay, choose the RV length under Type.

    Booking map

    Availability for the stay dates above. Green is open for the whole stay, yellow is partly booked, and red is booked. For an RV stay, choose the length so sites that are too short show in black. Click a green site to select it for this reservation. Drag to pan, scroll or +/− to zoom.

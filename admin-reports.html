All reservations

Type a name or member number, then choose the member. That shows every reservation for them. Clear the box to see everyone. Dates still narrow the list. Upcoming lists stays that start today or later. Completed means they checked in and the checkout date has passed. Past, in red, means the check-in day has passed and they never checked in.

    Current members

    Active and pending members. Closed memberships are left off. Password shows whether a sign-in password is set or is still a temporary password that must be changed. The site does not keep a readable copy of the password. Stays counts reservations that overlap the period you pick, not counting cancelled stays. Member since is the date the member record was created.
